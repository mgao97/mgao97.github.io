Min Gao

I am a Ph.D. student in the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Fudan University, advised by Prof. Yang Chen and Prof. Xin Wang. I received my M.S. degree in the College of Computer and Cyber Security at Fujian Normal University in 2021, advised by Prof. Li Xu.

My research interests include LLMs on graphs, effective graph analysis/learning, and real-world applications such as social bot detection and fraud detection.
